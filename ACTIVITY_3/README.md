## 🌊 Description

This activity focused on understanding global plastic pollution, its causes, effects, and possible solutions through an infographic and a proposed IoT-based Smart Waste Sorting and Plastic Pollution Monitoring System called **EcoSort**.

The project explored how technology, sensors, and monitoring systems can help improve waste sorting, monitor waste collection, promote responsible waste management, and reduce plastic pollution in communities.

## ♻️ Project Concept: EcoSort

**EcoSort** is a proposed IoT-based smart waste monitoring and sorting system designed to support more efficient waste management and environmental sustainability.

The concept explores how sensors and IoT technology can assist in waste monitoring, improve sorting practices, and help communities respond to plastic pollution more effectively.

---

## 📚 What Have I Learned?

- I learned more about the causes and environmental effects of plastic pollution, as well as the importance of proper waste management and recycling.
- I gained an understanding of how Internet of Things (IoT) technology, sensors, and monitoring systems can be applied to address real-world environmental problems.
- One challenge I encountered was organizing environmental information and translating the proposed technological solution into a clear and understandable presentation.
- I addressed this by structuring the information into problem, solution, and impact sections and using visual elements to communicate the concepts.
- In the future, I can apply what I learned by exploring technology-based solutions to environmental problems and improving my ability to present research through infographics and project proposals.
