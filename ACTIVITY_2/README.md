## 🎨 Description

This activity involved creating a visual identity for **FART2ART**, including a logo, color palette, typography set, and branding elements. The design used dark brown and light orange colors along with selected fonts to establish a consistent, distinctive, and recognizable visual style.

The goal was to develop a cohesive branding system that reflects the project's identity through thoughtful color selection, typography, and visual design.

## 📚 What Have I Learned?

- I learned the importance of branding and how colors, typography, and logos contribute to a business or project's identity.
- I gained experience selecting a color palette and fonts that work together to create a consistent and visually appealing design.
- One challenge I encountered was choosing design elements that would make the brand unique while maintaining a cohesive appearance.
- I addressed this by carefully combining a consistent color scheme, typography, and visual style throughout the branding materials.
- In the future, I can apply these principles when developing branding materials, logos, social media designs, advertisements, and other creative projects.
