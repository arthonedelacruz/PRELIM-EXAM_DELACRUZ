## 📄 Description

This activity involved using multiple Large Language Models (LLMs), specifically **ChatGPT and Claude**, to generate professional resumes based on the same personal information and qualifications. The generated resumes were then compared based on their structure, content, organization, clarity, formatting, and overall presentation.

The activity explored how different AI tools can assist in professional document creation while emphasizing the importance of critically evaluating AI-generated content to ensure accuracy, professionalism, and suitability for real-world applications.

## 🔍 Comparison Criteria

- Resume Structure and Organization
- Content Quality and Accuracy
- Professional Wording
- Formatting and Presentation
- Clarity and Readability
- Suitability for Professional Applications
- Adherence to Prompt Instructions

## 📚 What Have I Learned?

- I learned how Large Language Models can assist in generating professional documents such as resumes and how different AI tools can produce different results from the same prompt.
- I gained experience comparing AI-generated content and evaluating its organization, wording, formatting, and usefulness for professional applications.
- One challenge I encountered was determining which resume was more effective and professional while ensuring that the information remained accurate and appropriate.
- I addressed this by comparing the outputs based on their structure, clarity, content, and suitability for a professional nursing application.
- In the future, I can apply these skills when using AI tools for career preparation, resume writing, professional communication, and critically evaluating AI-generated content.
