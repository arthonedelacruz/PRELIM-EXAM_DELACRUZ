## ☕ About the Activity

This activity involved designing a promotional poster for a **Free Coffee Booth at Arrupe Booth 7**. The goal was to create an eye-catching and inviting design that effectively communicates the event details while encouraging students to visit and enjoy free coffee.

The poster incorporated creative visuals, typography, colors, and layout techniques inspired by the warm and cozy atmosphere of coffee.

---

## 🎨 Design Concept

> <span style="color:#6F4E37"><b>Warm. Inviting. Creative.</b></span>

The design was centered around a coffee-inspired aesthetic, using visual elements and design principles to capture attention and make the promotional message easy to understand. The combination of typography, layout, and colors helped create a poster that was both informative and visually appealing.

---

## 📚 What Have I Learned?

- I learned how to create an effective promotional design that communicates information clearly while capturing the audience's attention.
- I gained experience in selecting fonts, colors, and visual elements that complement the theme and purpose of an event.
- One challenge I encountered was arranging the text and design elements to make the poster visually appealing without overcrowding the layout.
- I addressed this challenge by prioritizing important information, balancing the visual elements, and using design components that complemented the coffee theme.
- In the future, I can apply these skills in creating promotional posters, advertisements, digital graphics, and other creative materials.
