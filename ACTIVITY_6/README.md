## 🏓 Description

This activity involved planning and developing a website concept for **BADINK**, a pickleball court business. It focused on establishing the website's objectives, including promoting the business, providing court information, enabling online reservations, displaying pricing, improving customer communication, and enhancing the overall customer experience.

The activity emphasized how a well-planned website can serve as a digital platform for businesses by providing convenient access to information, improving customer engagement, and supporting service accessibility.

## 🎯 Website Objectives

- Promote the BADINK pickleball court business.
- Provide customers with accessible court information.
- Display court pricing and available services.
- Support online reservations and booking convenience.
- Improve customer communication and engagement.
- Enhance the overall customer experience through an organized digital platform.

---

## 📚 What Have I Learned?

- I learned how a website can help promote a business, provide information, improve customer communication, and make services more accessible.
- I gained a better understanding of website planning, user experience, online reservations, business promotion, and the importance of having clear website objectives.
- One challenge I encountered was identifying the essential features and information that would make the website useful and convenient for customers.
- I addressed this by organizing the website objectives according to the needs of customers and the goals of the business.
- In the future, I can apply these skills when planning and developing websites for businesses and other projects, focusing on usability, accessibility, and customer satisfaction.
